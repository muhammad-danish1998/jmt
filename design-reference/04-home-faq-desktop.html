Who can take the Combined (Gap) exam?

Candidates with a gap of at least two years after passing Grade VIII (Combined SSC) or SSC (Combined HSSC) can appear as private candidates. Age limits and the pass year depend on the exam session, so check the page for your session: Annual (I) 2027 or Annual (II) 2026.

How long does it take to get my result?

Your result comes about two months after the exam. Exam and result dates are set by ZUEB.

Which documents do I need?

Every candidate needs a B-Form / CRC / CNIC copy and a recent formal photo with a blue or white background. Some categories also need a School Leaving Certificate or an SSC Marksheet & Certificate. Upload scans of the originals, because photocopies are not accepted. See documents by category.

I passed Matric from a board outside Sindh. What do I need?

You need a Migration Certificate / NOC issued in the name of the Ziauddin University Examination Board (ZUEB), along with your other documents.

What is the difference between Annual (I) and Annual (II)?

ZUEB holds two annual examinations, Annual (I) and Annual (II), and the eligibility rules differ for each. For example, Part I students who appeared in the Annual (I) 2026 exam are not eligible for Annual (II) 2026. Choose your session on the Eligibility page.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. We offer a merit scholarship based on your previous result, and need-based support with documents. Criteria and amounts are decided by JMT administration, so message our admissions team to apply.

Can I pay the fee in installments?

Yes, you can pay in installments. Message our admissions team on WhatsApp and we will arrange a plan that suits you.
