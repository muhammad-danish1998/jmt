Important board rules

Enrollment: Grade IX, Grade XI and Combined (Gap)

Document scans

Scanned copies of documents in their original condition must be uploaded on the portal. Photocopies are not acceptable.

Migration certificate

Migration certificates are required for students who passed Matriculation from boards outside Sindh Province. The Migration Certificate / NOC must be issued in the name of ZUEB.

Enrollment validity

The candidate's enrollment is valid as per the date mentioned on the enrollment card.

Objections and refunds

ZUEB can block or cancel any candidate's enrollment form if the school fails to rectify the Board's objection in four attempts. No refund will be made.

Combined (Gap)

One academic year

Combined (Gap) enrollment is valid for one academic year only.

Examination: Grade X, Grade XII and Failure / Absentees

Enrollment validity

The candidate's enrollment is valid as per the date mentioned on the enrollment card.

Examination form

ZUEB can withhold any candidate's examination form if the school fails to rectify the Board's objection. No refund will be made.

Improvement and Additional

One opportunity

Candidates are allowed one opportunity to appear in the Improvement Examination.

Renewal

An expired enrollment can be renewed for one more academic year.

Enrollment query? ZUEB helpdesk: helpdesk@zueb.edu.pk
