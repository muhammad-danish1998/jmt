FAQ

About this date sheet

Where is the official date sheet?

On the ZUEB website under Date Sheet. We link the official notice on this page. If our copy and the official notice ever differ, the official notice is correct.

What if ZUEB changes a date?

ZUEB can issue an updated schedule. We update this page and show the new "Last updated" date. Always check the official notice before your paper.

How do I know this session applies to me?

Eligibility rules differ for each session. Check your category on the Eligibility page first.

Need help with enrollment?

Tell us your class and the year of your last exam. We confirm your category and explain the next steps.
